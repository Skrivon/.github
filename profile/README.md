# Skrivon

Small, evidenced software products. Built in the UK.

## Thinwedge

**Finds the incumbent that can't cut its price.**

Thinwedge reads marketplace reviews and pricing pages across Atlassian, Shopify and WordPress, scores
every incumbent on six factors, and states why the vendor structurally can't respond to a cheaper
entrant. Every claim carries a date and a link to the page it came from.

[thinwedge.com](https://thinwedge.com) — in build, 12 September 2026

## How things get built here

**Evidence over adjectives.** Every claim a product makes carries a number, a date and a link to its
source. Findings, not opinions.

**Compliance is a build constraint, not a policy.** Crawlers honour robots.txt, never authenticate,
never circumvent a block, and never store reviewer identity. A 403 is a final answer.

**Prices are stable.** The price you sign at is the price you keep, and it says so in the contract.
